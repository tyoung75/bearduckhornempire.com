# Command Bars, Cardio Tracking, and a Domain Move

I spent most of this week making tools smarter and fixing things that annoyed me. The command bar in my task system finally understands what I mean when I type "delete this" or "snooze until Friday", Motus workouts now track runs and rides properly on the watch, and I moved the main BDHE domain after years of putting it off.

## Task System Gets Natural Language Commands

The command bar in my task management system could create tasks and search, but that was it. I kept trying to delete or snooze things by typing natural language commands and nothing would happen. So I shipped proper intent parsing.

Now it handles:
- "delete [task name]" or "remove this task"
- "mark [task] as done"
- "snooze [task] until Friday"
- "reprioritize [task] to P1"

I also expanded the database constraints to support multiple task sources beyond the old Linear import — now tracks `manual`, `cowork`, and `api` origins. This matters because I'm archiving Linear and consolidating everything into one system.

One small UX win: the High-Leverage Actions cards now have a dismiss button alongside Snooze and Done. Before, I had to mark irrelevant tasks as "done" just to clear them, which polluted the wins log. Now I can dismiss without marking completion.

## Motus Gets Real Cardio Support

Motus has been strength-first since day one, but I run 4-5x a week and needed proper tracking. This week I shipped end-to-end support for outdoor runs, outdoor cycling, and their indoor variants (treadmill, stationary bike).

Each workout type maps to the correct `HKWorkoutActivityType`, tracks GPS for outdoor variants, monitors heart rate, and syncs to HealthKit. I also added hiking and walking modes — hiking emphasizes elevation gain/loss, walking focuses on step count.

The bigger feature: **interval workout guidance**. For structured cardio sessions (think: 8x800m at threshold pace), the watch now shows the current interval, remaining time/distance, and previews the next interval. It auto-advances between work and rest phases with haptic feedback and alerts when pace drifts outside the target zone (configurable ±15 sec/mi tolerance).

I'm working toward Strava auto-upload next — workouts should sync to iPhone and push straight to Strava with GPS route, HR data, and activity type. Should be live in the next build.

Also shipped the watchOS app icon at all required sizes. Simple, bold mark that reads well at 24x24 and looks sharp on OLED black.
