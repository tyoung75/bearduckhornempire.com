## Nutrition Data Finally Matches Across Screens

I finally tracked down and killed a nutrition data bug in Motus that's been annoying me since I started using the app daily. The home screen and nutrition tab were showing different numbers for the same meals — not wildly different, but enough to make you second-guess whether your macro tracking was actually accurate.

Turns out the issue was in how I was aggregating supplement data versus meal data. The home screen was calculating totals one way, the nutrition detail view was doing it slightly differently, and when you had supplements mixed with regular meals, the discrepancy showed up. I unified the calculation logic so both screens pull from the same source of truth now.

This is one of those bugs you can't ignore once you notice it. If I'm going to trust my own nutrition app to track my intake, the numbers need to be bulletproof. Took about 50 commits worth of cleanup to get it right — lots of edge case testing with different combinations of meals, supplements, and serving sizes.

## Unit Selector for Supplement Servings

While I was in the supplement code, I added something that should've been there from day one: a unit selector for serving sizes. Before this week, if you logged a supplement, you were stuck with whatever default unit I'd hardcoded. Want to track creatine in grams but protein powder in scoops? Too bad.

Now you can pick the unit that makes sense for each supplement. It's a small quality-of-life thing, but it makes the supplement logging flow feel less rigid. I'm trying to make Motus work the way people actually track nutrition, not force everyone into one rigid pattern.

## What's Next

I'm diving into prompt validation improvements for the meal logging flow. Right now if you enter something ambiguous like "chicken" without specifying cooked/raw or the cut, the LLM has to guess. I want to catch those cases earlier and prompt for clarification before we even hit the nutrition API. Should make the data quality better and reduce the back-and-forth when logging meals.

Also considering adding meal templates — save common meals you eat regularly and log them with one tap. That's a bigger feature though, so we'll see where it lands in the priority queue.
