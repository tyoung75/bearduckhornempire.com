## The Data Mismatch I Finally Fixed

You know that feeling when you know something's wrong but can't quite pin it down? For weeks, I'd noticed that Motus sometimes showed different nutrition totals on the home screen versus the detailed nutrition tab. Not every time. Not for every user. Just... sometimes.

This week I finally hunted it down. The problem was in how I was aggregating nutrition data from different sources — meals, individual foods, and supplements were all being summed differently depending on which view you were looking at. The home screen was using a snapshot calculation, while the nutrition tab was doing a live query that included more recent data.

The fix wasn't complicated once I found it: standardize on a single data aggregation function that both views call. But tracking it down required adding a bunch of logging, creating test cases with known totals, and stepping through the flow to see where the numbers diverged.

Now both views pull from the same source of truth. Totals match. Crisis averted.

## Making Supplement Tracking Less Annoying

While I was in the nutrition code, I tackled something that's been on my list forever: unit selectors for supplement servings.

Before this week, if you wanted to log a supplement, you had to enter a number and hope the default unit matched what you meant. Want to track vitamin D in IU? Too bad, here's milligrams. Tracking fish oil in grams but the bottle lists it in milliliters? Good luck with the mental math.

Now you get a proper unit selector when editing supplement servings. Pick from common units (mg, mcg, g, IU, ml), and Motus remembers your preference for that specific supplement. It's one of those tiny changes that makes the app feel less like fighting with a form and more like just logging your data.

## Prompt Validation Improvements

I also cleaned up some validation logic around user inputs. Nothing dramatic — mostly making sure edge cases don't break things. Empty strings where they shouldn't be, weird Unicode characters, that sort of thing.

The goal is to catch bad input before it gets into the system rather than exploding later when something tries to process it. Boring infrastructure work, but it prevents those head-scratching bugs that show up three layers deep in the stack.

## What's Next

Now that the nutrition data is solid, I'm focusing on the meal planning side of Motus. The current flow for building a meal plan is clunky — too many taps, too much back-and-forth between screens. I want to experiment with a more streamlined approach where you can drag foods into a plan directly from your recent meals or favorites.

Also planning to add basic recipe support. Right now if you eat the same combination of foods regularly, you have to log each one separately every time. Being able to save "turkey sandwich with avocado" as a single recipe would save a ton of time for repeat meals.
